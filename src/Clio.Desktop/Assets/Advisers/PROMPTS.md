# Adviser portraits — paper studies

Release: horizon-33. Generated using the built-in image_gen tool, September 10, 2026. These are new portraits inspired by Michael's grounded Cromblog doodles, not scans presented as original drawings by Michael.

The four PNGs beside this file replace the earlier painted portraits. The executable embeds them under the unchanged resource names, so economic, military, cultural and social advice use the new art throughout the game. AdviserPortraits.cs draws a square paper frame with a fine ink edge.

## Style references

From the local Cromblog workshop source (`typescript/cromblog`):

- `art-source/auspicious-august-and-a-signal-september/doodle-fidelity-review.png`: natural raven and wren studies; loose lines and open paper.
- `art-source/2025-06-2026-01/review/page-091.jpg`: only the lower-left bearded human profile (`jj2526-p091-c`); human proportions and beard strokes. Modern clothing, other figures and writing are excluded.
- `art-source/2025-06-2026-01/review/page-101.jpg`: mountain study (`jj2526-p101-a`); directional hatching and stronger contours.
