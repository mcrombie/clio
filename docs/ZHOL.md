# Zhol — founding vocabulary, first edition

Zhol is Michael's personal language for Clio. Its name is **Zhol**: the beginning of *zhong* and the *ol* of *español*. Mandarin supplies the initial Chinese sound inspiration. New games and the New Story dialog select Zhol by default; other languages remain available.

The word-making rule is to bring together a Chinese and a Spanish sound cue for the same idea, then shorten and smooth the result into a word that feels good to say. A blend should sound like one word. The first edition often keeps a Chinese opening and a Spanish ending, with soft **r/l**, **sh/zh**, and the vowels **a e i o u**. These are authored inventions; the cues guide taste rather than imposing a mechanical spelling formula.

The founding people are called **Zholhen**. Their language is **Zhol**. A custom band name changes the people’s name without renaming the language. Place-name recipes use these roots, and daughter languages inherit and transform them through the game's sound-change system. Borrowing retains the recipient's language name.

This edition supplies the game's twelve naming roots. **Culture → Language** shows the founding vocabulary beside possible descendants; it is not a sentence or grammar generator. Root spellings use plain Latin letters so the existing map labels, inheritance and loan-adaptation system can carry them consistently.

## Persistence and future whims

Zhol is appended to the founding-template choices, preserving the identity of every earlier option. Saved stories retain their recorded language; loading an older story does not turn it into Zhol. New Story returns to Zhol as its default even after loading an earlier culture.

New stories explicitly record **Zholhen** as their founding people name, including when the dialog's name field is blank. Earlier blank-name saves retain the released **Herio-rente** recipe when replayed. This keeps old history and names reproducible without a save-format change; custom people names remain authoritative.

The first vocabulary is explicit in the simulation's Zhol founding data and stays the same across world seeds. Treat released roots as replay content: later changes to this edition need an explicit content revision or migration so existing Zhol stories can reproduce their original names. New concepts should follow the same fusion principle, with the final sound chosen to Michael's taste.
