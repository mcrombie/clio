# Diplomacy

Diplomacy is a main tab between Units and History. Its page body starts empty, beneath the page heading.

The first recorded separation of a band from your tribe opens the relationship list. With dispositions enabled, the timing depends on that band's personality and distance: a Separatist can leave after three missed closes when two or more hexes away, while a Loyal household has eighteen. Forming a daughter band keeps it in your tribe and does not open Diplomacy; neither temporary drift nor unrelated starting peoples qualify. The trigger comes from the game's visible secession record, so loading a story with an existing separation restores it without a new unlock command. [Current tribal deadlines](TRIBES.md).

After that first separation, the page lists independent living polities whose bands are currently on explored land. When none are observed, previously recorded gatherings remain readable; a story without such records retains the empty page. Multiple observed bands of the same polity share one record. Only their observed members contribute current names, positions and population totals; Whole Atlas does not expand this information.

Select a people to read:

- **Neutral** when no feud with your tribe is recorded, or **Enemy** when an existing feud makes the tribes hostile.
- **Neighbor** when an observed band shares or adjoins one of your households' hexes, otherwise **Further afield**.
- Recorded descent from your tribe and the turn of separation, where known.
- Population and band counts within observed land.

Shared ancestry does not automatically create friendship or hostility. This initial page reflects the current relationship rules; negotiations, treaties, friendship and rivalry systems remain future work.

**Find their band** pauses autoplay and opens a currently observed representative's map record. It preserves your commanded household, rechecks visibility and independence, and spends no action. The relationship list has five rows per page. Viewing records, changing selection and navigating pages do not alter the simulation, reveal land or change relations.

**Relationship**, **Gathering** and **Record** subviews keep inspection separate from paid choices. Known peaceful splinters can now receive an invitation, travel to a physical meeting, receive a gift of food or salt and agree a return visit. Each choice has an exact preview and explicit confirmation. The [gathering rules](GATHERINGS.md) describe costs, attendance, adviser disagreements and V10 persistence. Larger negotiations and treaties remain future work.
